Our Story

More than just a cleaning company.

DS Cleaners was founded over 15 years ago with a straightforward mission: to provide the kind of thorough, trustworthy cleaning service we'd want for our own home. What started as a small domestic operation has grown into a full-service company trusted by hundreds of homes and businesses.

We still operate with those same family values at our core — honesty, hard work, and genuine care for every client. Every team member is personally trained, vetted, and shares our commitment to excellence.
